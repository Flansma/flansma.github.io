## Overview

Poster at the CBI Society Annual Meeting 2026, presented by Kyoka Iyanaga. Stochastic structure predictors find a correct antibody–antigen complex more often with more runs, but at higher cost; this work tests whether confidence available after only a few runs can drive an adaptive decision to stop or continue.

**Session:** P06 "Drug Discovery Application" (P06-26, core time B: Oct 27 17:00–18:00 / Oct 28 16:00–17:00)

**Affiliation:** Kyoto University Graduate School of Medicine, Department of Biomedical Data Intelligence
